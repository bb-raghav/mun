01 About RNSIT MUN

A room for the
world’s next voices.

Model United Nations turns global affairs into a shared exercise in listening, research and negotiation.

Delegates represent countries or assigned roles, explore complex questions and work together on considered proposals. Every speech starts with preparation. Every resolution starts with dialogue.
