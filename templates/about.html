RNSIT MUNSoc is a student society at RNS Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. Established in 2023, it creates space for students to explore world affairs, debate and diplomacy.

The Society brings students together to practise research, public speaking, negotiation and attentive listening.

Through MUNs, quizzes, training programmes and discussions, members engage with international affairs and different perspectives. The club describes a community of more than 30 members.
