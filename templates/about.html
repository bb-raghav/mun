01 Our purpose

Listen with intent.
Lead with ideas.

RNSIT Model United Nations is a student forum for exploring international affairs and the practice of diplomacy.

Through committee simulations, delegates research the positions of assigned countries or roles, exchange ideas and work toward thoughtful proposals. This site is a prototype; specific conference details and institutional information can be added once verified.

A shared simulation

What is
Model United Nations?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an educational simulation of diplomatic forums. Participants represent countries or other assigned roles, study an agenda, debate policy and collaborate on written outcomes. It offers a structured way to practice public speaking, research, negotiation and respectful disagreement.
